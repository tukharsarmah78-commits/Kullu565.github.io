Original Assamese Play Stage Script

'মকৰা জাল' (Mokora Jaal)

Written by Tushar Sharma (Kullu)

Play Synopsis

'মকৰা জাল' (The Spider's Web) delves into the invisible webs of modern human expectations, interpersonal relationships, and internal conflicts faced by youth in contemporary Assamese society.

[নাটকৰ অংশ - Excerpt from Act 1 Scene 2]

"প্ৰত্যেকটো শব্দৰ আঁৰত এখন অদেখা জাল থাকে... তুমি নজনাকৈয়ে সেই জালখনত বন্দী হৈ পৰা।"

- (English translation: "Behind every word lies an unseen web... without knowing, you get entangled within it.")

'মকৰা জাল' (Mokora Jaal) Script Overview

Written by Tushar Sharma (Kullu)

Theme & Concept

The play explores how humans build invisible nets of expectations around themselves. Set in modern Assam, it contrasts traditional family values with modern individualistic ambitions.

[চৰিত্ৰ - Key Characters]
  • অৰ্ণৱ (Arnab): The reflective protagonist wrestling with artistic aspirations.
  • প্ৰণৱ (Pranav): A pragmatic friend grounded in conventional realities.

দ্ৰুপদ - Scene Excerpt

"মই ভাবিছিলোঁ জালে মানুহক বন্ধ কৰে। কিন্তু এতিয়া বুজিছোঁ, মানুহে নিজে বনোৱা বিশ্বাসবোৰেই আটাইতকৈ ডাঙৰ জাল।"
